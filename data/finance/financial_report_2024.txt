FinSolve Technologies achieved record-breaking financial performance in 2024,
crossing the $50 million annual revenue milestone for the first time in company
history. Net profit margins improved to 18.4%, up from 14.2% in 2023, driven
by strong SaaS subscription growth and operational efficiency initiatives.
