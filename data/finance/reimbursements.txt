REIMBURSEMENT POLICY OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FinSolve Technologies reimburses employees for legitimate business expenses
incurred in the performance of their duties. All claims must be submitted
within 30 days of the expense using the Expensify platform with receipts.

APPROVAL THRESHOLDS:
  Up to $500      →  Direct Manager approval
  $500 – $2,000   →  Department Head approval
  $2,000 – $5,000 →  VP / CFO approval
  Above $5,000    →  CEO approval required

REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS & TIMELINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Step                              Timeline
  ────────────────────────────────  ──────────────────────────────
  Employee submits in Expensify     Within 30 days of expense
  Manager review & approval         2 business days
  Finance audit                     3 business days
  Payment processing (bank xfer)    Next payroll cycle (bi-weekly)
  Total typical turnaround          7–14 business days

PAYMENT METHOD: Direct bank transfer to employee's registered account.
CURRENCY: All claims in INR. Foreign expenses reimbursed at RBI reference
          rate on date of transaction.
